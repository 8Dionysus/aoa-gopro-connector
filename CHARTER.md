# Charter

`aoa-gopro-connector` exists so an operator-owned GoPro can become a reliable,
portable, and evidence-bearing sensor/actuator capability for agents without
losing human authority over physical effects, media, privacy, or retention.

The repository should grow toward:

- capability discovery that is exact to camera, firmware, API, and topology;
- one leased camera actor with reconciliation and bounded recovery;
- explicit read, candidate, approval, effect, and receipt contracts;
- integrity-preserving preview, recording, Hilight, and selective media ingest;
- an optional typed GoPro Labs capability pack;
- provider-independent semantic sensing and capture policy;
- measured multicamera orchestration rather than implied perfect sync;
- independent installation plus admitted AbyssOS deployment.
