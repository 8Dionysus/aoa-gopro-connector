# Roadmap

## Now — Phase 0

- Establish the independent repository, package, schemas, CI, validation, and
  fake/replay boundary.
- Preserve a sanitized HERO13 Black stock-firmware USB/NCM capability baseline.
- Keep the only live route read-only: camera info, state, and media inventory.
- Record SDK/runtime compatibility and unresolved BLE, Wi-Fi, preview, record,
  GPMF, and recovery questions without promoting them to readiness.

## Next — control, transport, stream, and product surface

- Build composed state, lease, operation plan/receipt, actor, reconciliation,
  cancellation, deadlines, and fault recovery.
- Prove USB/NCM control/media first, then BLE wake, Wi-Fi AP, and COHN as named
  topology profiles.
- Prove preview, recording, Hilight, media finalization, resumable ingest, and
  checksums on named hardware profiles.
- Stabilize CLI and loopback daemon contracts for independent use.

## Later — federation and expanding capability

- Admit host prerequisites and long-running deployment through their owners.
- Admit read/candidate before an exact approved-effect bridge.
- Add a firmware-profiled typed GoPro Labs compiler and QR renderer.
- Add semantic provider ports, selective capture, and an accepted finger-snap
  scenario.
- Add independent actors, partial-result semantics, and measured alignment for
  multiple cameras.
- Close with compatibility, endurance, security, release, owner review, and
  Operator acceptance.
